## Where I got stuck, if I did

Was it the RULES OF THE SYSTEM being unclear, or HOW TO MODEL them? Answer
that one explicitly, even if the answer is "both, in this order". It decides
whether the problem gets fixed or left alone.

## How you handled time

This is the decision the problem is really about, so write it down before
anything else. A lease expires, and nothing in the statement says whether
that needs a clock. Say what you reached for first, whether you changed your
mind, and what it cost you.

## What I tried and dropped

Representations you considered and rejected, and why. This is the most
valuable thing in the log, because it is the only direct evidence of whether
the problem admitted real modeling choice.

## The properties, and where each one held

The ask was to check every property against both versions. Record which ones
survived the second version and which did not, including any that surprised
you. A property that failed where you expected it to pass is worth more here
than one that behaved.

## Anything that read as broken rather than hard

Ambiguity, a rule you had to guess at, a contradiction. Not "this was
difficult". That is a non-event.

The rules were checked against a worked model before delivery and no
contradiction was found, so if one reads wrong it probably is. One thing is
known and deliberate: rule 3 admits more than one reading, and the wording was
left alone because tightening it starts naming events.
