# Pneumatic tube

A bank teller sends capsules to the vault through a pneumatic tube. A
capsule is red or blue. The teller puts a capsule in at her end whenever
she likes, and the vault clerk takes one out at his end whenever one has
arrived. Capsules come out in the order they went in.

Write the specification from an empty file. State the rule in the last
sentence so that TLC can check it. Keep to the two colours and stop the
model once four capsules have gone in. Say how you held it there.

Check it. A correct model finds 129 distinct states.
