# Dispatch board

A taxi office keeps a board of waiting calls in the order they came in.
Each call is from one of three districts: north, south or east. The board
has three lines. When the police close a district, the dispatcher strikes
every call from that district off the board at once, and the calls that
remain keep their order. No call is taken from a closed district. A closed
district is reopened later and its calls resume. The office answers calls
too, and that is left out here.

Write the specification from an empty file. Say what the board can be.
State the rule that the board never shows a call from a closed district.

Check it. A correct model finds 98 distinct states.
